About Me

I'm Jhonel Vertudazo, an IT Specialist, SEO Specialist, Web Developer, and Drone Operator dedicated to helping businesses turn technology and digital opportunities into measurable results.

With a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from North Eastern Mindanao State University – Tagbina Campus, I combine technical knowledge, problem-solving, creativity, and business thinking to develop solutions that are practical, reliable, and built around real-world needs.

My work sits at the intersection of technology, websites, search visibility, and digital content. I don't simply build digital solutions—I focus on understanding the problem behind the project and creating solutions that help businesses operate better, reach more people, and grow.

SEO Specialist
